FAQ

Do I need to switch my billing or membership software?

No, keep using Kicksite, Spark Membership, Zen Planner, or whatever you already use! Your website links right to its sign-up, booking, and member portal pages, so nothing changes for your students or staff.

Can I update the schedule myself?

You don't have to! Just text or email me the change (even a quick photo of the schedule on your whiteboard works) and I'll take care of it. Every change is included, and I make small changes within 3 days and bigger ones within 5.

How does the members-only section work?

It's simple! Each group gets its own password, like a rank, your competition team, or your instructors. Students use it to open their group's pages: curriculum, terminology, technique videos, private photo galleries, tournament info, or lesson plans. It's one shared password per group, so it's for class material, not private records. Want to change a password? Just let me know! Try the demo →

Do you only build sites for karate schools?

No, I build for any martial arts or combat sports school!

I already have a website. Can you redesign it?

Yes, I'd love to! That's exactly what I did for AABBA Karate and Usami Rika Dojo. If you already own your domain, you keep paying for it, and I'll move it over and connect it to your new site, so your web address stays the same.

Who owns the website?

You own your domain name, business name, logo, and the photos, videos, and business information you give me. I own the website's design and code, including its layout, styling, and site files.
